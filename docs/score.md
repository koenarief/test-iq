# SISTEM PERHITUNGAN REKRUTMEN HRD

### 1. ASSESSMENT SCORE

Penilaian awal kandidat menggunakan 3 instrumen:

**Assessment Score = (DISC × 20%) + (IST × 25%) + (Competency × 55%)**

**Minimum Score Gate:**

* DISC ≥ 60
* IST ≥ 60
* Competency ≥ 65
* Assessment Score ≥ 70

**Jika seluruh syarat terpenuhi → PASS**
**Jika salah satu tidak terpenuhi → FAIL / NOT RECOMMENDED**

---

### 2. JOB FIT SCORE

Mengukur kesesuaian kandidat dengan kompetensi yang dibutuhkan pada posisi.

**Job Fit Score = Σ (Nilai Kompetensi × Bobot Kompetensi)**

Bobot kompetensi **disesuaikan dengan masing-masing posisi**.

Contoh Sales Executive:

* Sales Orientation = 30%
* Communication = 25%
* Problem Solving = 15%
* DISC = 15%
* Cognitive/IST = 15%

**Total Bobot = 100%**

---

### 3. STRENGTH & DEVELOPMENT AREA

|    Nilai | Kategori         |
| -------: | ---------------- |
|     ≥ 90 | Exceptional      |
| 80–89,99 | Strong           |
| 70–79,99 | Adequate         |
| 60–69,99 | Development Area |
|     < 60 | Weak             |

Hasil digunakan untuk mengidentifikasi **Strength** dan **Development Area** kandidat.

---

### 4. INTERVIEW SCORE

**Interview Score = Σ (Nilai Aspek Interview × Bobot)**

Contoh bobot:

* Communication = 20%
* Motivation = 15%
* Problem Solving = 20%
* Job Knowledge = 20%
* Attitude = 15%
* Work Alignment = 10%

**Total Bobot = 100%**

---

### 5. FINAL SCORE

Setelah kandidat lolos Assessment Gate:

**FINAL SCORE = (Assessment × 50%) + (Job Fit × 20%) + (Interview × 30%)**

Hasil akhir berada pada skala **0–100**.

|  Final Score | Keputusan       |
| -----------: | --------------- |
|     **≥ 85** | RECOMMENDED     |
| **75–84,99** | CONSIDER        |
|     **< 75** | NOT RECOMMENDED |

---

### ALUR SISTEM

**CANDIDATE**
↓
**DISC + IST + COMPETENCY**
↓
**SCORING ENGINE**
↓
**ASSESSMENT SCORE**
↓
**MINIMUM SCORE GATE**
↓
**PASS → JOB FIT → STRENGTH & DEVELOPMENT AREA**
↓
**INTERVIEW**
↓
**FINAL SCORE**
↓
**RECOMMENDED / CONSIDER / NOT RECOMMENDED**

### PRINSIP SISTEM

Sistem menggunakan **scoring terukur, minimum score gate, bobot sesuai posisi, dan final score** untuk membantu HR melakukan proses seleksi secara **konsisten, objektif, dan terdokumentasi**.
